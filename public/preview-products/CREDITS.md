# Sample product photos (settings previews only)

These eight photographs are used **only** to draw the sample storefront in the Wishlist Settings and Notify Me Settings
previews (`app/utils/preview-products.js`). They are not product data, are never uploaded to a shop and are never linked to a
customer's Wishlist.

All were downloaded from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) at 640 px wide (2026-11-02), each from a photo page marked
**"Free Photo"** (not Unsplash+). They are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use,
including commercially, no attribution required. Each page names the photographer; the source page is kept here so the
photo can be traced. Photographs were chosen to show no brand logo or printed text, and a single product with no person.
